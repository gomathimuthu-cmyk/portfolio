my story

Every child has their own way of learning

Children learn best when they feel safe, valued, understood and encouraged to explore.

My journey in education began in a classroom in Chennai, where I learned that teaching isn't simply about delivering a lesson. It's about understanding the learner, creating the right environment, and finding ways to help each child participate and grow.

Over the last 9+ years I've worked across schools, NGOs, EdTech and education programmes — teaching, designing curriculum, supporting teachers, improving academic quality and looking after learners' wellbeing.

Every one of those roles kept pulling me back to the same thing: the individual child. That's what led me to specialise in Early Years Education and Special Educational Needs — play-based learning, child development, inclusive practice and children's wellbeing.

…and I'm now ready to bring that to a new setting, anywhere in the world.

Every child deserves to belong

Inclusion isn't an extra activity — it's in how we plan, teach, communicate and support.

Learning should be joyful

Children learn through hands-on play, exploration and the freedom to experiment.

Every child is different

There's no single approach that works for everyone. Support should be individual.

Relationships come first

Children thrive when they feel safe and connected to the adults — and families — around them.

Watch, then act

Observation shows me each child's interests, strengths, challenges and next steps.

Grow confidence, not just skills

Communication, independence and wellbeing matter as much as any learning goal.

How I make one activity work for different children

My aim isn't a separate activity for every child — it's making the same experience accessible to all of them.

💬 Communication

  • Visual prompts
  • Simple, clear instructions
  • Modelling & repetition
  • Alternative communication

🖐️ Sensory

  • Choice of materials
  • Reduced stimulation
  • Quiet spaces
  • Gradual introduction

🎯 Attention

  • Shorter steps
  • Movement breaks
  • Clear routines
  • Visual schedules

✂️ Motor skills

  • Adapted tools
  • Larger resources
  • Physical support
  • Other ways to finish

💛 Emotional

  • Predictable routines
  • Positive reinforcement
  • Emotional check-ins
  • One-to-one time
